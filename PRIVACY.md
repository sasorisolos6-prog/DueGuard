# DueGuard Privacy & Data Guarantee

1. **Try Without an Account:** Local Demo mode stores items strictly in your browser (`localStorage`) and never contacts any server.
2. **Private Vault Storage:** Uploaded invoice and warranty screenshots are stored in private Cloudflare R2 storage and served only after verifying your authenticated session owns that specific tracker. No public links are generated.
3. **Zero Ad Tracking or AI Training:** Your deadlines, ₹ spend figures, and receipts are never sold, shared with advertisers, or used to train third-party AI models.
4. **Notification Channels:**
   - **Email:** Sent only when you enable Email Alerts.
   - **Telegram:** Linked only when you send `/start <code>` to the bot; unlinking removes your `telegram_chat_id` immediately.
   - **Android App:** Local reminders run offline on your device using Android notifications.
5. **No Lock-In:** Use **Export JSON Backup** or **Export Calendar (.ics)** at any time, or **Delete Account & Wipe Vault** to permanently erase all database records and uploaded images.
